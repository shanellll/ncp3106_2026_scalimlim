ABOUT CpE

Welcome to the Computer Engineering Organization. We bring students together through technology, innovation, projects, and activities.

CpE AT UE

The Computer Engineering program provides students with knowledge and skills in computer hardware, software, electronics, networking, and modern computing technologies.

SPECIALIZATIONS

Computer Engineering covers different areas where students can develop their technical knowledge and skills.

EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Designing computer systems that are built into electronic devices.

INTERNET OF THINGS

Connecting devices and sensors to communicate and exchange data.

NETWORKING

Designing and managing computer networks and communication systems.

SOFTWARE

Developing programs and applications that solve different problems.

CYBERSECURITY

Protecting computer systems, networks, and information from security threats.

ROBOTICS

Combining hardware and software to create automated machines and systems.

CAREERS

Computer Engineering graduates can pursue different careers in technology, engineering, software, hardware, and networking.

COMPUTER ENGINEER

Designs, develops, and maintains computer-based systems.

SOFTWARE ENGINEER

Develops and maintains software applications and systems.

EMBEDDED SYSTEMS ENGINEER

Develops software and hardware for embedded devices.

NETWORK ENGINEER

Designs, configures, and maintains computer networks.

HARDWARE ENGINEER

Works with the design and development of computer hardware.

IoT ENGINEER

Develops connected devices and Internet of Things systems.
