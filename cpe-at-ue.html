COMPUTER ENGINEERING AT UE

The Computer Engineering program at the University of the East prepares students to understand and develop modern computing technologies.

PROGRAM OBJECTIVES

The program develops skills in hardware, software, programming, electronics, computer systems, networking, and technological innovation.

SKILLS AND EXPERIENCES

Students gain experience through laboratory activities, programming projects, hardware development, embedded systems, and other computer engineering applications.
