ABOUT
CpE AT UE

Computer Engineering deals with the application of engineering principles in the analysis, design, implementation, and management of hardware and software. The UE CpE program combines electrical engineering and computer science to help students understand both sides of the hardware-software interface.

HARDWARE Circuits, computers and embedded systems
SOFTWARE Programming and operating systems
INTEGRATION Hardware + software solutions
