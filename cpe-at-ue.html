SEPTEMBER 2025 ACHIEVEMENT

UE Computer Engineering Warriors Clinch 1st Runner-Up at PacketHacks: Hack the Climate 2025

Representing UE Manila at the IoT Conference Philippines 2025, five BSCpE students earned 1st Runner-Up in the national Packetworx PacketHacks. Guided by Engr. Errol John M. Antonio, students Sean Vincent Vien V. Viñas, Clarizza M. Reyes, Skhart Aye D. Mercado, Paolo B. Barrado, and John Paul A. Pascual developed AeroBand—a blockchain-integrated personal air quality monitoring device. Competing against over 70 teams, they showcased technical skill, innovative problem-solving, and commitment to UN SDGs in UE's debut at the hackathon.

APRIL 2026 HIGHLIGHT

Celebrating Innovation: 1st Computer Engineering Research Colloquium-Forum

The Computer Engineering Department made history on April 24, 2026, with its inaugural Research Colloquium-Forum and Design Project Exhibit. Bringing together 3rd-year design proposals and 4th-year completed systems, the event showcased student projects evaluated by faculty and experts based on technical merit, innovation, and SDG alignment. Featuring interactive prototypes and formal presentations, the landmark colloquium celebrated academic excellence.

2026 ENGAGEMENT

Sophomore CPE Students at Arduino Day Philippines 2026

Sophomore Computer Engineering students took part in Arduino Day Philippines 2026, gaining hands-on exposure to electronics, embedded systems, and technology-focused activities. The event provided an opportunity for students to explore practical applications of Arduino and connect classroom learning with real-world engineering experiences.

OCTOBER 2025 COMPETITION

Sharp Minds and Quick Logic: Top Winners Honored at Circuit Minds Quiz Bee

On October 7, 2025, eight teams of Computer Engineering students spanning 1st through 4th year competed in "Circuit Minds: The CPE Quiz Bee Competition." Testing logic, speed, and technical knowledge, the event celebrated teamwork, academic excellence, and student participation while recognizing the top three winning teams.

AUGUST 2025 RESEARCH

UE Computer Engineering Team Named Finalists in PUP Research Awards

The UE Manila Computer Engineering Team was named among the Top 5 research papers in the Innovation Category of PUP's 15th Annual Research Awards. Their machine learning-enhanced robotic system for strawberry harvesting showcased the research and innovation of UE's Computer Engineering program.

APRIL 2026 EXHIBIT

Design Project Exhibit at Research Colloquium 2026

The Research Colloquium 2026 featured design projects created by 3rd and 4th year students. Interactive prototypes and research posters showcased how student ideas developed into actual systems while inspiring younger students to explore future project possibilities.
